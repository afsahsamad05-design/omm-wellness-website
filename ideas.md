# OMM Wellness Website Design Direction

## Three initial approaches

### Theme Name: Warm Ritual House
**Very Brief Intro:** A tactile, editorial wellness identity built around warm stone, linen, olive leaf, and quiet confidence. It makes at-home treatment feel like a considered ritual rather than a commodity booking.
**Probability:** 0.07

### Theme Name: Desert Modern Sanctuary
**Very Brief Intro:** A sun-washed UAE-inspired system using sand, clay, shadow, and architectural spacing. It positions OMM as a contemporary destination for private, discreet, high-touch care.
**Probability:** 0.03

### Theme Name: Soft Clinical Balance
**Very Brief Intro:** A calm, premium treatment-led system combining ivory, sage, and ink with precise service information. It creates trust through clarity while still feeling human and restorative.
**Probability:** 0.08

## Chosen approach: Warm Ritual House

### Design Movement
Modern editorial wellness with Japanese-inspired restraint and the tactile material language of a boutique spa: linen, oiled wood, river stone, olive leaf, and quiet serif typography.

### Core Principles
1. **Ritual over rush:** Every section should slow the eye down while making the next action obvious.
2. **Warm precision:** Service information, benefits, and booking details are direct, specific, and easy to scan.
3. **Material softness:** Use warm neutrals, fine rules, paper-like surfaces, and photography with visible texture rather than glossy gradients.
4. **Visible path to value:** Home spa booking, training enrollment, and careers are separate revenue paths with distinct calls to action.

### Color Philosophy
The palette is built around charcoal ink, mineral cream, warm sand, olive smoke, and one ownable terracotta accent. Cream creates hospitality, charcoal provides trustworthy contrast, olive signals grounded wellbeing, and terracotta gives every conversion moment a human pulse without turning the site into a generic luxury template.

### Layout Paradigm
Use an asymmetric editorial flow: a split hero with image and statement, staggered service cards, narrow reading columns beside oversized whitespace, and side-by-side action panels for booking and training. Avoid a repeated centered-card grid; content should move like a printed wellness journal with clear conversion rails.

### Signature Elements
- Fine terracotta rules and small uppercase labels that frame each section like a treatment note.
- Offset image crops with soft paper borders and tactile shadows, echoing a spa treatment tray.
- A recurring “Balance / Life / Freedom” rhythm used as a compact brand signature, never as filler copy.

### Interaction Philosophy
Interactions should feel like a calm invitation: buttons lift slightly, navigation glides to sections, service cards reveal more detail without aggressive motion, and booking actions remain persistent and visible. WhatsApp is the fastest route; email and training forms are secondary but clear.

### Animation
Use 180–260ms ease-out transitions for buttons, cards, and navigation states. On first view, stagger small labels, headlines, and image blocks by 40–60ms. Reveal service details through opacity and translateY only. Respect prefers-reduced-motion and keep the floating WhatsApp action static except for a subtle hover scale.

### Typography System
Use Cormorant Garamond for display headlines and wordmark-like editorial moments; use Manrope for navigation, service facts, buttons, and body copy. Headlines are large, light-to-medium weight with generous line-height; body text is 15–18px with comfortable measure; labels use 11–12px uppercase tracking.

### Brand Essence
**OMM Wellness brings discreet, therapist-led home spa care and practical training to people who want wellbeing to fit beautifully into real life.** Personality: grounded, discerning, nurturing.

### Brand Voice
Headlines are calm and sensory, CTAs are specific and low-friction, and microcopy reassures rather than oversells. Avoid vague promises and fabricated proof.

Example lines:
- “Turn your home into a private sanctuary.”
- “Choose your treatment. We bring the ritual.”

### Wordmark & Logo
Use an abstract, bold three-line “OMM” mark inspired by three stacked river stones or three measured breaths, paired with the OMM WELLNESS wordmark in a refined serif/sans lockup. The mark must also work alone as the favicon and WhatsApp-adjacent booking cue.

### Signature Brand Color
**Terracotta Ritual — #B86F54.** A muted clay tone that makes the brand feel warm, human, and memorable without relying on overused blush or purple gradients.

## Style Decisions — redesign

The site has intentionally moved away from Warm Ritual House into **Mediterranean Signal**: bright mineral white and mist-blue surfaces, cobalt blue structure, citrus-lime conversion accents, and a sharper contemporary hospitality feel. Typography now pairs DM Serif Display with Manrope. Generated hero, treatment, training, careers, and abstract mark assets are used where available; the fallback treatment image replaces any failed generated placeholder. Preserve the architectural image frames, electric cobalt section breaks, citrus highlights, and direct booking-first composition in future revisions.


## Style Decisions — Original Oam Identity

The website now follows the original Oam The Therapist reference: the verified olive circular OAM logo is the primary brand mark; the palette uses olive, charcoal, white, soft gold, and warm clay; typography pairs an elegant italic Cormorant Garamond display face with a restrained DM Sans body face; and the recurring signature is “Balance | Life | Freedom.” The visual system intentionally replaces the previous cobalt-and-lime direction so the logo and interface read as one coherent brand.
